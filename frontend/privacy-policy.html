Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how SmartDine may collect, use and protect information when you use our platform.

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

SmartDine is a multi-restaurant online food ordering platform. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when customers visit our website, create an account or use our services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may collect:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and account details.
  • Delivery address and order information.
  • Food preferences and customization choices.
  • Feedback, reviews and support messages.
  • Basic technical information about website usage.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process and manage food orders.
  • Provide order updates and delivery information.
  • Personalize food recommendations.
  • Support allergy-related ingredient information.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Respond to customer questions and feedback.

4. Information Sharing

Information necessary to fulfil an order may be shared with the relevant restaurant or delivery service. Information may also be shared with service providers when required to operate platform features.

SmartDine should not sell customers' personal information as part of its intended platform functionality.

5. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate security measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage or sessionStorage to remember preferences, maintain cart information and support website functionality.

7. AI-Based Features

SmartDine includes planned AI-based features such as diet recommendations, allergy information support, dish customization and review analytics.

Recommendations are intended to assist customers. They should not replace professional dietary or medical advice. Customers should verify ingredient and allergen information directly with the restaurant.

8. Third-Party Services

Some platform features may depend on external services such as payment gateways, maps or other integrations. Their data practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

9. Your Choices

Customers can review or update available profile information through their account settings. For questions about personal information, customers can contact the SmartDine support team.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when platform features or data practices change. Updated information will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.

Email: Contact SmartDine Support
